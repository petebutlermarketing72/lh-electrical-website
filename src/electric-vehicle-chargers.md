A properly installed EV charger is faster, safer, and far more convenient than running a lead through a window or relying on public charging. We're MCS-certified for EV installations and handle most home installs in a single visit, once the site survey is done.

## What's included

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Site survey — usually a short visit, or photos and your consumer unit details</li>
<li>Charger supply and installation (tethered or untethered)</li>
<li>Dedicated circuit and, where needed, consumer unit upgrade</li>
<li>Commissioning and certification</li>
<li>Commercial and multi-unit installs</li>
</ul>

## Choosing the right charger

Most modern chargers are vehicle-agnostic, so the brand of your car matters less than you'd think. The more useful questions are whether you want a tethered or untethered unit, and what smart features or app control you actually need. We'll talk this through before quoting, rather than upselling features you won't use.
