<p>A properly installed EV charger is faster, safer, and far more convenient than running a lead through a window or relying on public charging. We're MCS-certified for EV installations and handle most home installs in a single visit, once the site survey is done.</p>

<h2>What's included</h2>
<ul>
<li>Site survey — usually a short visit, or photos and your consumer unit details</li>
<li>Charger supply and installation (tethered or untethered)</li>
<li>Dedicated circuit and, where needed, consumer unit upgrade</li>
<li>Commissioning and certification</li>
<li>Commercial and multi-unit installs</li>
</ul>

<h2>Choosing the right charger</h2>
<p>Most modern chargers are vehicle-agnostic, so the brand of your car matters less than you'd think. The more useful questions are whether you want a tethered or untethered unit, and what smart features or app control you actually need. We'll talk this through before quoting, rather than upselling features you won't use.</p>

<h2>Pair it with solar</h2>
<p>Sussex enjoys more sunshine than much of the UK, so pairing your charger with <a href="/solar-panels/">solar panels</a> means you can charge your car for free during sunny periods, on top of the lower running costs a home charger already gives you over public charging.</p>

<h2>Common questions</h2>

<h3>Do I need an EV charger from the same brand as my car?</h3>
<p>No. Most modern chargers work with any electric vehicle. The better question is tethered vs. untethered, and what smart features matter to you.</p>

<h3>What's the lead time on an install?</h3>
<p>Once the site survey is done, installation is typically within one to two weeks, sometimes sooner.</p>

<h3>Will my consumer unit need upgrading?</h3>
<p>Sometimes — we'll check this during the survey and include it in the quote if needed, rather than as a surprise on the day. See our <a href="/electrical-services/fuse-board-upgrades/">fuse board upgrades page</a> for typical pricing.</p>
