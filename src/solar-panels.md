<p>As an MCS-certified installer, we handle the electrical side of solar PV and battery storage installations — from the initial assessment through to commissioning and certification. MCS certification matters because it's what makes you eligible for schemes like the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG), and it's what most insurers and mortgage lenders expect to see.</p>

<h2>What we cover</h2>
<ul>
<li>Solar PV installation — electrical side</li>
<li>Battery storage installation and commissioning</li>
<li>Consumer unit and metering upgrades where needed</li>
<li>MCS certification for scheme eligibility</li>
<li>Domestic and commercial installs</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why MCS certification matters</h2>
<p>MCS-certified installers are independently assessed against national standards for solar and renewable installations. Beyond the paperwork, it means your system is installed to a consistent standard, that you're covered for scheme eligibility, and that the certification will be recognised if you come to sell the property.</p>

<h2>Pair it with an EV charger</h2>
<p>Solar and <a href="/electric-vehicle-chargers/">EV charging</a> work well together — charge your car for free during sunny periods, and get more out of the electricity your panels generate rather than exporting it at a lower rate than you'd pay to import.</p>

<h2>Common questions</h2>

<h3>Do I need planning permission for solar panels?</h3>
<p>Most residential rooftop solar installations fall under permitted development, but it depends on your property and location. We can advise during the survey.</p>

<h3>Can I add battery storage later?</h3>
<p>Yes, in most cases a battery can be added to an existing solar installation. We'll assess your current setup to confirm compatibility.</p>

<h3>What's the benefit of the Smart Export Guarantee?</h3>
<p>It means you get paid for excess electricity you export back to the grid. MCS certification is a requirement to register for it.</p>
