A rewire doesn't have to mean total upheaval. We survey the property first, agree a clear scope of work, and plan cable routes to keep disruption to the smallest sensible intervention — whether that's a full rewire or targeted replacement of specific circuits.

## When a rewire is worth considering

<ul class="checklist">
<li>The property hasn't been rewired since before the 1980s</li>
<li>An EICR has flagged the existing installation</li>
<li>You're renovating and want the electrics designed in properly</li>
<li>Rubber-insulated or lead-sheathed cable is present</li>
<li>Frequent nuisance trips or an undersized consumer unit</li>
</ul>

## How we approach occupied homes

Rewiring a house you're still living in is different from rewiring an empty shell. We plan the work in stages where possible, lift floorboards or run discreet surface conduit depending on the property, and keep you informed at each stage so there are no surprises.

## Common questions

<dl class="faq">
<dt>How long does a full rewire take?</dt>
<dd>It depends on the size and layout of the property, but most domestic rewires take between one and two weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline as part of the quote.</dd>
<dt>Can we stay in the house during the work?</dt>
<dd>Often, yes — we plan room by room where possible. For some properties a phased approach works better than others; we'll be upfront about what's realistic for your home.</dd>
<dt>Will I need to redecorate afterwards?</dt>
<dd>Some making-good is usually needed around chased walls or lifted floorboards. We keep this to a minimum and will flag likely areas before starting.</dd>
</dl>
