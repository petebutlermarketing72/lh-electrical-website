Most domestic jobs fall into one of a few categories: something's stopped working and needs fixing, something needs upgrading to current regulations, or you're planning a renovation and need the electrics designed in from the start. We handle all three, with a survey and fixed written quote before any work begins.

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Fault-finding and repairs</li>
<li>Consumer unit and fuse board upgrades</li>
<li>Full and partial rewires</li>
<li>Additional sockets, circuits, and outdoor power</li>
<li>Lighting upgrades and smart lighting</li>
<li>Garden offices, outbuildings, and extensions</li>

## Why it's worth getting it right

Electrical work that's rushed or done cheaply tends to show up later — as nuisance trips, as a failed EICR when you come to sell, or worse. We're NAPIT-registered, which means our notifiable work is certified and registered with your local authority, and every job comes with the paperwork you'd want to see if you ever needed to prove it was done properly.
