Uckfield has grown faster than almost anywhere else in East Sussex over the last decade. New estates around Ridgewood and Hempstead Lane sit alongside Victorian terraces near the High Street and a working industrial estate at Bellbrook — each with its own electrical priorities. We're based in Lewes and travel up to Uckfield several times a week, working on the full spread from new-build EV installs to commercial periodic inspections.

## The Uckfield jobs we see most often

A pattern has emerged in the new-build estates: residents move in, settle for a year, then add the things the developer didn't — a proper EV charger instead of relying on a granny lead, smart lighting, dedicated circuits for a garage conversion or a garden office. We're MCS-certified for EV charger installs and handle most of these jobs in a single visit.

In the older parts of town — particularly the streets off the High Street and around Framfield Road — the conversation tends to start with a fuse board that's tripped one too many times, or with an EICR ordered ahead of a sale or remortgage. Both are routine work for us, both come with the certification your solicitor or insurer will want to see.

## A growing town, growing electrical demand

The shift to electric vehicles has been more visible in Uckfield than in most of the towns we cover — probably because so many residents commute to Lewes, Brighton, Tunbridge Wells, or further. A home charger pays back quickly against public charging, and a properly installed unit is faster, safer, and a great deal more convenient than running a lead through the kitchen window.

## Common questions

<dl class="faq">
<dt>Do I need an EV charger from the same brand as my car?</dt>
<dd>No. Most modern chargers are vehicle-agnostic. The better question is whether you want tethered or untethered, and what app and smart features you actually need. We'll talk it through before quoting.</dd>
<dt>What's the lead time on an install?</dt>
<dd>Once we've done the site survey — usually a short visit, or a few photos and your consumer unit details — installation is typically within one to two weeks, sometimes sooner.</dd>
<dt>We're a small business at Bellbrook — can you handle our periodic inspection?</dt>
<dd>Yes. Commercial periodic inspections are a regular part of our work. We'll quote based on circuit and distribution board counts, schedule around your trading hours where possible, and provide the certificate you need for insurance and compliance.</dd>
