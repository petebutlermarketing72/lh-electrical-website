## Landlord electrical compliance, done properly

Since 2020, private landlords in England have been legally required to hold a current EICR — every five years, or at change of tenancy. Penalties for getting it wrong are real (up to &pound;30,000), but the bigger frustration we hear from landlords is patchy quality of reports. We provide an EICR that's actually useful: a clear classification, a written report you can hand to your letting agent, and, where remedial work is needed, a fixed quote to bring the property up to standard without spinning out the timeline. For portfolio landlords and letting agents, we'll coordinate inspections across multiple Hailsham properties to keep void periods short.
