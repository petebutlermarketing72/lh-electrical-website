      <div class="split-content reveal reveal-delay-2">
        <p>LH Electrical (South East) Ltd was founded by Luke Hartery, who started out taking on domestic jobs across Lewes and the surrounding villages. What began as a one-man operation has grown steadily on reputation and word of mouth &ndash; still family-run, still answering the phone ourselves.</p>
        <p>Today, Luke works alongside George and Finn, taking on domestic, commercial, solar and EV work across Lewes, Ringmer, Uckfield, Hailsham, Peacehaven, Seaford and the surrounding villages.
