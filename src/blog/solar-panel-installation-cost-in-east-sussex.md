<li>Roof type, size and orientation</li>
<li>Battery storage capacity</li>
<li>Smart energy features (e.g. solar + EV charging integration)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Are There Any Solar Grants or Incentives in 2025?</h3>
<p>Yes — while upfront grants have reduced, you can still benefit from:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>0% VAT on solar installations</strong> (until 2027)</li>
<li>The <strong>Smart Export Guarantee (SEG)</strong> – get paid for excess electricity sent back to the grid</li>
<li>Potential local council incentives (varies by area)</li>
</ul>

<h3>How Long Does It Take to Break Even?</h3>
<p>Most systems <strong>pay for themselves in 6 to 9 years</strong>, with some customers breaking even faster—especially if paired with EV charging or battery storage.</p>
<p>And because most panels last <strong>25+ years</strong>, that's <strong>15–20 years of free, clean energy</strong> after your system has paid for itself.</p>
