<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>LH Electrical completed an integrated solar PV, battery storage and electric vehicle charging installation designed to help a homeowner generate, store and manage electricity more effectively.</p>
<p>The installation combines roof-mounted solar generation with a Tesla Powerwall, Backup Gateway and dedicated EV charging equipment.</p>

<h2>System Specification</h2>
<ul>
<li>5.5kW flat-roof solar PV system</li>
<li>Tesla Powerwall battery storage</li>
<li>Tesla Backup Gateway</li>
<li>Whole-home backup capability during grid outages</li>
<li>Tesla EV charger</li>
<li>Surge Protection Device (SPD)</li>
</ul>

<h2>Generating and Storing Solar Electricity</h2>
<p>The solar PV system generates electricity during daylight hours.</p>
<p>The Tesla Powerwall allows compatible excess generation to be stored for use later, helping the homeowner make greater use of the electricity generated at the property.</p>

<h2>Backup Power During Electricity Outages</h2>
<p>The Tesla Backup Gateway supports the system's backup functionality, allowing the configured home installation to continue supplying electricity during grid interruptions, subject to system capacity and operating conditions.</p>

<h2>Combining Solar, Battery Storage and EV Charging</h2>
<p>By bringing together solar generation, battery storage and EV charging, the installation provides a more integrated approach to home energy management.</p>
<p>Actual savings and grid independence depend on electricity consumption, tariffs, generation and battery use.</p>
