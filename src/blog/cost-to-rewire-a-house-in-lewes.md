<h2>What affects the cost to rewire a house?</h2>

<h3>1) Property size &amp; circuit count</h3>
<p>Bigger homes mean longer cable runs, more circuits (kitchen, cooker, sockets, lighting, bathrooms, outdoor, EV prep), and more accessories. As a rule of thumb, <strong>each extra bedroom adds ~£1,000–£1,500</strong> to the budget.</p>

<h3>2) Age &amp; condition of the installation</h3>
<p>Pre-1960s wiring (rubber or fabric-insulated) or older consumer units often demand full replacement and improved earthing/bonding. Period homes (common in Lewes) can add <strong>£1,000–£2,000</strong> due to careful routing and making good.</p>

<h3>3) Empty vs occupied</h3>
<p>Works are faster in empty properties. If you're living in during the rewire, allow <strong>20–30%</strong> extra time and <strong>£1,000–£1,500</strong> for the added making-safe and clean-up each day.</p>

<h3>4) Walls &amp; floors</h3>
<p>Suspended timber floors and plasterboard walls are simpler. Solid brick walls, lath-and-plaster ceilings and concrete floors take longer, commonly adding <strong>£500–£1,500</strong>.</p>

<h3>5) Specification choices</h3>
<p>White plastic accessories and a standard 18th Edition metal consumer unit keep costs lean. Upgrades like decorative metal plates, smart controls, USB sockets or extensive downlighting typically add <strong>15–25%</strong>.</p>

<h3>6) Access &amp; location</h3>
<p>Central Lewes is usually straightforward; rural access can add modest travel time. External works in conservation areas may need a sympathetic approach and occasionally approvals.</p>

<h2>Do you actually need a rewire?</h2>

<h3>Age &amp; condition checkpoints</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Very likely needs rewiring:</strong> wiring older than ~40 years; rubber/fabric/lead-insulated cables; round-pin sockets.</li>
<li><strong>Probably needs significant upgrades:</strong> 30–40 years old; no RCD protection; failed or poor EICR results.</li>
<li><strong>Could be a partial rewire:</strong> 20–30 years old with isolated issues or when renovating specific rooms.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Warning signs to act on</h3>
<ol>
<li>Regular breaker trips or blown fuses</li>
<li>Flickering/dimming lights</li>
<li>Scorch marks or discolouration on sockets/switches</li>
<li>Burning smells or buzzing</li>
<li>Shocks or tingles from metalwork</li>
<li>Warm outlets or melted plug pins</li>
</ol>

<p>If you're seeing any of these, book a safety check on <strong>01273 443324</strong>.</p>
