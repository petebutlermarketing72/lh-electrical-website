An old fuse board — particularly one with rewireable fuses or no RCD protection — is one of the more common reasons we get called out. Upgrading to a modern consumer unit improves safety, reduces nuisance trips, and is often required before other work (like an EV charger or extra circuits) can be added safely.

## Signs your fuse board needs attention

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Rewireable fuses instead of circuit breakers</li>
<li>No RCD or RCBO protection</li>
<li>Frequent tripping, especially with multiple appliances running</li>
<li>An EICR has flagged the board as unsatisfactory</li>
<li>You're adding circuits (EV charger, extension, outbuilding) and the board is full</li>
</ul>

## What's involved

Most upgrades are completed in a day, with a planned power cut during the work. We'll talk you through what's being replaced, fit a board with individual RCBO protection per circuit where appropriate, and provide the certification you need once it's done.
