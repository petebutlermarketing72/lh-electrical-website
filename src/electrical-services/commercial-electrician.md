We work with small businesses, landlords, and letting agents who need electrical work done reliably and scheduled around trading hours. ## What we cover

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Commercial periodic inspections (EICRs)</li>
<li>Fit-outs for retail, hospitality, and office units</li>
<li>Three-phase installations and distribution board work</li>
<li>Emergency lighting and fire alarm circuits</li>
<li>General maintenance and fault-finding</li>
<li>Solar PV and EV charger installs for commercial premises</li>
</ul>

## Scheduling around your business

We know that downtime costs money. Where possible, we schedule commercial work outside trading hours, or in phases that let you keep operating. For multi-site businesses or letting agents managing several properties, we can coordinate inspections together to keep things efficient.
