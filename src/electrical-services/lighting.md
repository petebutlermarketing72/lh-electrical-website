Good lighting is as much about layout as it is wiring — where switches sit, how a room is zoned, what needs to be dimmable. We work through this with you before anything's installed, whether it's a straightforward LED upgrade or a smart system with app and voice control.

## What we cover

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Domestic lighting upgrades and new installations</li>
<li>LED conversions and energy-saving retrofits</li>
<li>Outdoor, garden and security lighting</li>
<li>Smart lighting with app or voice control</li>
<li>Office, retail and commercial lighting schemes</li>
<li>Support for Rako, Lutron, Lightwave and similar smart systems</li>
</ul>

## Getting the layout right

The most common regret we see with lighting is switches and circuits that don't match how a room's actually used. We'll talk through zoning and dimming at the planning stage, so the finished result works for the space rather than just lighting it.
